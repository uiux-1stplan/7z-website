The Philosophy

Designing Silla Hall as a Landmark

Every space in this presentation was approached the same way the 7Z Magic team approaches any palace-grade venue: not as a renovation, but as a landmark in the making. The goal was never to simply brighten a photo — it was to ask what Silla Hall could become if every arch, wall, and threshold were pushed to its most cinematic, most timeless version of itself.

That meant locking the camera and the architecture in place — the boundary walls, the staircase geometry, the dance floor footprint — so every option could be judged purely on its design merit, side by side with the original. Light became the main storytelling tool throughout: golden hour warmth at the gate, blue hour drama at the staircase, and a fully choreographed glow across the main stage.

The result is a venue that reads as one coherent identity from the first gate to the final dance floor — palace-grade materials, a restrained luxury palette, and lighting designed to feel timeless in every photograph, at any hour of the event.

01 — LANDMARK DESIGN

Instant Recognition

Every space is composed for a dual day-and-night identity, so Silla Hall is immediately recognizable in any light, from any angle.

02 — CINEMATIC LIGHT

Light as Storyteller

Golden hour, blue hour, and full night treatments were staged deliberately — light does the emotional work in every frame.

03 — MATERIAL HONESTY

Palace-Grade Materials

Stone, brass, and marble were kept true to how they behave in real light — nothing reads as an effect or a filter.
