## 1. Diagnosis

**The business.** Multi-specialty medical centre, Ras Al Khaimah, founded 2007. MOHAP licence 5080. Six doctors. Revenue mix has migrated from dental-only into dermatology, injectables, laser hair removal, non-surgical aesthetics and laboratory. Contracted with 18+ insurers (volume base) while selling discretionary self-pay aesthetics (margin base).

**Binding constraint: price realisation, not awareness.** They are established, licensed, insured, publicly recognised and already advertising. What they cannot currently do is *look like* the price tier their aesthetic line needs to charge at.

**Presenting problem.** "We want a new, modern, attractive identity look."

**Structural problem.** Three separate failures compounding:

1. The 2020 Sukoun identity was **never fully rolled out** — multiple application pages in their own brand book are still stamped *"Pending Approval"* six years later.
2. The identity was built for a **dental clinic**; the P&L is now a medical-aesthetic centre. The mark says *smile*; the growth is in skin.
3. The physical expression has **drifted into three unrelated décor languages** — emerald/gold lounge, rustic blossom-and-birdcage feature room, pale damask corridor — plus wholly unbranded clinical rooms.

**Consequence.** The environment reads cheaper and older than the price list. In a credence category, the buyer's evidence disagrees with the number, so price resistance and discount pressure follow — and the aesthetic line, which is where the margin is, is the line most exposed to it.

**The killer evidence (use this in the room).** Their own About page states the vision: *"patients feel they've entered a healing spa, with a luxurious interior that makes them feel safe, comfortable, and cared for."* We are not selling them our taste. We are selling them **their own stated intention, undelivered.**

## 4. Why 3,000 JOD is underpriced

Four independent checks, all pointing the same way:

1. **Against 7Z's own benchmark.** The approved Tawjihi pricing runs ~73% margin. 3,000 JOD here runs 43.8%. Same studio, same month, materially worse deal.
2. **Against the client's market.** 3,000 JOD ≈ **15,530 AED**. In the UAE, a bilingual custom website alone commonly quotes in the 15,000–35,000 AED band. This bundle adds an identity system, 24 concepts and three commercial films — and would still sit at or below the price of the website component alone.
3. **Against the client's own spend.** They already pay for: a Dubai brand studio (Sukoun, Business Bay), three professionally produced campaign audio tracks at 256–320 kbps including a full three-minute Ramadan production, professional interior photography, print advertising in *Municipalities & Union* magazine, a WordPress/WPML site, and 18+ insurance integrations. **This is not a low-budget buyer.**
4. **Against price-as-signal.** We are selling a *premium visual standard* into a credence category. A number that reads cheap actively undermines the argument that we can deliver premium. Underpricing here is not merely lost margin — it is a positioning contradiction.

**Resolution:** 3,000 JOD is not deleted, it is **repositioned as the bottom of the grid** (the Essential tier). This satisfies the brief's target while making it reachable only by *dropping scope*, never by discounting — exactly per `7z-pricing-engine` doctrine.

## 11. Presentation strategy

**Lead with:** the diagnosis, using their own materials as evidence — the "Pending Approval" stamps and their own About-page vision quote. This is the highest-trust opening available and it is unarguable because it is all their own material.

**Then:** the concepts. Dr. Nael needs to *see* it.

**Then:** the commercial spine, delivered without hedging. Hanan needs structure, not enthusiasm.

**Hold back as secondary:** the expansion roadmap, the seasonal campaign thinking. Mentioning them early makes the current scope feel like a foot in the door.

**What will create resistance:** any suggestion that the clinic currently looks bad, or that the logo needs work. Never denigrate. The frame is always *"catching the expression up to the achievement."*

**Detail level:** high for Hanan, visual for Nael. The document deliberately serves both.
